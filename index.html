How it is built

Medallion architecture: clean data by design

Bronze · Source truth

What did the system actually contain?

  • Raw data, as received
  • Full audit trail to the source
  • No business logic applied

Silver · Core

Is it clean, consistent, and complete?

  • Standardized fields and signs
  • Customers and products de-duplicated
  • Reconciled to GL and control reports

Gold · Sales Cube

What does it tell us?

  • Analysis-ready facts and dimensions
  • Standard KPIs and definitions
  • Feeds Excel, dashboards, and apps
Nexus Quality checks run at every layer: record counts, control totals, duplicates, missing values, unexpected signs, period completeness, and a documented bridge from source to deliverable.

Who it is for

Companies that may transact in the next 1–3 years

  • Lower-middle-market companies that depend on spreadsheets for reporting
  • Owners who know the business well but cannot quickly show it with data
  • Data spread across ERP or QuickBooks, billing, CRM, and operating systems
  • Companies that want to show buyers durable revenue and strong customer relationships

The problem

“We're doing well, but I can't prove it quickly.”

Many companies cannot easily answer:

  • Which customers drive growth, and which ones are we losing?
  • Which products and services earn the best margins?
  • How much of our revenue is recurring?
  • Will our customer data reconcile to our financial statements?

Deliver insight. Management dashboards, monthly KPI packages, and Excel reports.
Get transaction-ready. Prepare sell-side analytics in the format buyers and bankers expect.
